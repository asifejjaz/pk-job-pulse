How this works

SourcesIndeed and LinkedIn: every Pakistan posting from the last 24 hours, all sectors and seniority levels, scanned once a day.
DeduplicationThe same opening posted on both sites counts once, keyed on company, title and city. Each post counts on the day it first appeared.
ClassificationSector, seniority and city come from rules on the job title, because the sites leave those fields empty. About 17% of titles are too vague to place.
FiltersEvery number, chart and table on this page is recounted from the same data when you change a filter, so the parts always add up.
Company tagsSteady: posted on at least 4 days and a third of scan days. Burst: 5 or more posts with 70% on one day. New: first ever post in the last 7 days of the period.
RecruitersAgencies post for other employers. They are detected by name and kept out of the company ranking unless you pick them under Posted by.
RemoteUses the remote flag the job site provides. Some remote roles are not flagged, so treat remote shares as a floor.
LimitsSalary is not published on almost any post, so pay is not covered. Companies that never post on these two sites are invisible here.
PrivacyOnly counts are published: no job links and no descriptions. A job title appears only when several companies share it.
